Vocab → Notion

Right-click a word and its meaning anywhere, and it lands in your Notion page.

1 Notion integration token

  1. Open notion.so/my-integrations → New integration.
  2. Give it a name (e.g. “Vocab”), pick your workspace, and create it.
  3. Copy the Internal Integration Secret and paste it below.

Stored locally on this computer only — the token is never put in Chrome Sync and only ever sent to api.notion.com.

2 Destination

Paste the link to the Notion page you want words appended to — or to a database, if you prefer one row per word.
  1. Open that page in Notion → ••• → Connections → add your integration.
  2. Copy its link (••• → Copy link) and paste it below.

3 How entries look

Saving into a database instead? The word goes in the title property, and the meaning, URL and date fill the first matching Meaning / URL / Date properties.

Feedback while saving

Chrome blocks in-page confirmations on its own pages (settings, the Web Store); a system notification is used there regardless.
